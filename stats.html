About these numbers

Detailed statistics have only been collected since the Spring 2026 season. Minutes played, starts, assists, goals against, cards, plus-minus and MVP awards exist for Spring 2026 onwards, and there is no equivalent record for the seasons before it.

Goals are recorded for every season the club has played: Winter 2024/25, Winter 2025/26, Spring 2026 and the current Winter 2026/27, including cup rounds. For the earlier seasons, goals are the only statistic we have.

A dash means a player was not with the club that season. Former players are marked and stay on the all-time list.
