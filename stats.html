About these numbers

Detailed statistics have only been collected since the Spring 2026 season. Matches, minutes, starts, assists, cards, plus-minus and MVP awards exist from Spring 2026 onwards. For Winter 2024/25 and Winter 2025/26 the club recorded goals only.

In the club career table, a dash means we have no detailed record for that player — they played only in the seasons before this data was collected. Goals, however, are complete for every season.

Cup matches count towards the career totals alongside league games. Former players stay on the list.
