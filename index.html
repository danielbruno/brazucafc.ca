Our story

Founded in 2024. Growing every season.

Brazuca FC was founded by a group of Brazilians with one simple idea: bring people together through football and compete in the Fraser Valley Soccer League.

Season after season we’ve grown on and off the field — a stronger squad, better organization, new partners — without losing the friendship and team spirit that started it all.
